3:T6d9,<p>The origins of eigenstudio can be traced back to a question that emerged long before the company itself was incorporated. Why does a nation capable of designing nuclear reactors, launching satellites, developing advanced defence systems, and producing world-class engineers continue to depend on foreign engineering simulation software for some of its most critical technological decisions?</p>
<p>The story dates back to 2010. Following their graduation from the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Raipur in 2010, Abhinav Gupta joined the Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL), while Kamal Shanker Patel joined Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL). Working within two of India’s most important strategic sectors exposed them to a fundamental gap in the country’s engineering ecosystem: India had the talent and capability to build nuclear reactors but did not have its own globally competitive engineering simulation software. In organisations such as NPCIL, this dependence became even more critical when sanctions, licensing restrictions, and limited access to foreign software could directly affect engineering and research workflows. That realisation led to a simple question: why not build our own?</p>
<p>But building advanced simulation software requires much more than software development. It demands deep expertise in computational mechanics, numerical methods, applied mathematics, and scientific computing. That understanding shaped the next phase of the journey. Abhinav and Kamal moved into doctoral research in Computational Mechanics, spending years developing numerical algorithms, publishing research, and building the scientific depth required to eventually create indigenous computational technologies.</p>
