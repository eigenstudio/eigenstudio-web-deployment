<p>At eigenstudio, we leverage advanced electromagnetic simulation technologies, computational methods, and engineering expertise to solve challenging design problems across industries including telecommunications, automotive, aerospace, electronics, energy, and defense.</p>
<h2>Introduction</h2>
<p>Modern products operate in environments where electromagnetic interactions are unavoidable. Signals, currents, magnetic fields, and electromagnetic waves continuously influence system behavior, often affecting performance in ways that are difficult to predict through physical testing alone.</p>
<p>As devices become smaller, faster, and more interconnected, electromagnetic effects play an increasingly important role in system design. Engineers must address challenges such as signal integrity, electromagnetic interference, power losses, antenna performance, and electromagnetic compatibility throughout the product development lifecycle.</p>
<p>Simulation-driven engineering enables teams to visualize field distributions, predict system behavior, and optimize designs before manufacturing begins. This significantly reduces development risks while accelerating innovation and improving overall product performance.</p>

<p>By combining physics-based modeling with high-performance computing resources, engineers can evaluate complex electromagnetic interactions that would otherwise require extensive experimental testing. This approach enables faster design iterations and more informed engineering decisions.</p>
