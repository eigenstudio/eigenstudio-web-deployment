The challenge

Engineering is fragmented

Engineering today is disconnected, manual, and fragmented. Disconnected workflows cause rework, slow iterations, and months-long cycles.

Specification

Requirements are captured separately from the wider engineering design workflow.

System Model

Designers recreate and modify models across multiple disconnected engineering tools.

Simulation

Analysis requires repeated setup, simulation runs and design revisions across iterations.

Validation

Results return through reviews, design changes and repeated engineering iterations.

Certification

Final evidence is assembled separately for manufacturing, certification and approval.
